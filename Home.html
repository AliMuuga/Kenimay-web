About Us

Kenimay Engineering began as a general engineering and jobbing company in 2013. Over the years, we have undertaken numerous challenging jobs, gaining expertise in machining, problem solving, building trades, and engineering projects.

Today, Kenimay Engineering takes on mechanical engineering projects of all sizes, providing fitting, turning, machining and many more engineering services. Our core principle is "Quality over Quantity" — ensuring clear communication, follow-ups, and customer satisfaction at every stage.
